[h1]Sovereign Arsenal — Regent Expansion[/h1]
[b]Sixteen weapons, a combat style of your own on every climb.[/b]

An expansion that lets the Regent Forge more than a greatsword.

From crystal blades and staves to rifles and trajectory cannons, from Sovereign Wings to the Codex of Light: choose your weapon, strengthen it with Forge during combat, and build around its unique strengths.

[h2]Choose Your Weapon Before the Climb[/h2]
After Neow's event, the Regent visits the Departure Armory. Choose how to equip yourself for this run:
[list]
[*][b]Choose a Weapon[/b]: Select a weapon from four categories and make it your Forge weapon for the run.
[*][b]Choose the familiar weapon[/b]: Forge the original Sovereign Blade and claim its own Tempering upgrades.
[*][b]Let Fate Decide[/b]: Randomly select one weapon and use it throughout the run.
[*][b]Master of All Arms[/b]: Whenever Forge needs to create a weapon, one is selected at random.
[/list]
The armory includes categories, back navigation, and automatic pagination. Weapon selection is now part of the opening event; no mod-settings weapon pool setup is needed.

[h2]Sixteen Weapons, Four Categories[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Light[/b]: Sovereign Crystal Blade, Sovereign Dagger, Neptune's Trident, Imperial Katana, Sea Calming Staff.
[*][b]Heavy[/b]: Sovereign Scythe, Sovereign Greataxe, Sovereign Warhammer, Sovereign Spear, Sovereign Shield.
[*][b]Ranged[/b]: Dread Emperor's Sacred Bow, Royal Rifle, Galactic Trajectory Cannon.
[*][b]Special[/b]: Sovereign Wings, Codex of Light, Royal Knuckles.
[/list]
Cycle through the staff's three Stances, charge your cannon, receive Blessings from the Codex, or become a Martial Fanatic with Royal Knuckles. Each weapon has different effects, Forge benefits, and decisions to make in combat.

[h2]Weapon Tempering: Grow Your Arsenal as You Climb[/h2]

Forge a weapon that suits your combat style, then unlock new strengths along the way.

Open the Royal Task Graph from the Weapon Tempering button near the map icon in the top-right corner. Defeat the Act I and Act II bosses to claim Tempering I and Tempering II, unlocking upgrades unique to each weapon. The 16 mod weapons and the original Sovereign Blade offer 34 main-path rewards in total.

Shrink your enemies with the Warhammer, strike again with the Crystal Blade, fire an extra shot when the Rifle exhausts a Bullet, or recover Energy as the Staff cycles through its Stances. Choose a weapon and plan your build around its two stages of upgrades.

An independent side task, Jade Tempering, rewards you for winning 5 elite combats with +4 base attack damage for the corresponding weapon. It requires no main-path rewards, giving elite routes their own opportunity for growth.

Browse weapons, pan and zoom the graph, and click a node to see its task and reward. Choose a Weapon and Let Fate Decide let you upgrade the weapon selected for your run; Master of All Arms lets you claim upgrades for each weapon separately. Upgrades take effect when claimed. Progress is saved with your run and resets when you start a new one.

[h2]New Opening Choice: Choose the familiar weapon[/h2]

Prefer the Regent's original Sovereign Blade? Select Choose the familiar weapon in the Departure Armory and keep Forging the original greatsword. It has its own two Tempering rewards: a lower base Energy cost, followed by shared base damage growth for all your Sovereign Blades whenever one scores a kill.
